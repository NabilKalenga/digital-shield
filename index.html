KNOW THE RISKS

Common Online Dangers

Understanding online threats is the first step toward protecting yourself.

🎣

Phishing

Fake messages and websites designed to trick people into giving away personal information.

👤

Fake Accounts

People may pretend to be someone else online in order to deceive others.

🔑

Weak Passwords

Easy-to-guess passwords can make accounts easier to access without permission.

📱

Unsafe Apps

Some apps or websites may request information they don't really need.

STAY SAFE

Protect Yourself Online

Small habits can make a big difference when you're using the internet.

🔐

Use Strong Passwords

Use strong, unique passwords for important accounts.

🛡️

Protect Personal Information

Think carefully before sharing personal information online.

🔎

Check Before You Click

Look carefully at links, messages and websites before clicking.

🤝

Ask for Help

If something online makes you uncomfortable or confused, talk to a trusted adult.

SPOT A SCAM

Can You Recognize a Scam?

Scammers often use messages that create urgency, excitement or fear.

🚨 Warning Signs

  • ⚠️ Someone demands immediate action.
  • 🎁 You are promised something that seems too good to be true.
  • 🔗 You receive a suspicious link.
  • 🔑 Someone asks for your password or security code.
  • 💳 Someone asks for sensitive payment information.
